## A family business, in reliable hands

**Net-Trade Hungary Kft.** has decades of experience in manufacturing wooden
pallets, crates and packaging materials, as well as the professional packaging
of industrial machinery and equipment. As a family business, we believe that
**our client's success is our success**.

We design our solutions to meet the strict requirements of sea, road and air
transport, so our partners can rest assured their products are safe when
shipped to any corner of the world.

## What we do

- **Industrial packaging** – export-proof packaging of machines, production lines and equipment.
- **Woodworking** – custom and standard pallets, crates, mass-produced timber goods and structures.
- **International freight forwarding** – road transport by articulated lorry.
- **Agency trade** – representation of Slovak sawn timber and the BRITTERM brick factory.
- **Grants and training** – development funding and professional courses.

## Packaging by mode of transport

- **Road:** crane-movable and open-crate constructions.
- **Sea:** VCI corrosion protection and moisture barriers against container humidity.
- **Air:** weight-optimised packaging with VCI protection.

On request we also supply timber **heat-treated and IPPC-marked to ISPM-15**
standards for international transport.

## Where our shipments have arrived

Our experience spans **four continents**: we have shipped to Hungary and beyond
to the **USA, Brazil, Vietnam and South Africa** – that is, beyond Europe to
America, Asia and Africa.
