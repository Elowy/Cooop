## Delivery and pickup

We deliver orders placed in our webshop throughout the country, and personal
pickup is also available at our premises. Below is a summary of the most
important information about shipping.

## Delivery methods

- **Home delivery by courier** – to the shipping address you provide, on working days.
- **Personal pickup** – at our premises: 2660 Balassagyarmat, Mártírok útja 72, Hungary.
- **Pallet / oversized delivery** – for larger quantities by dedicated transport, arranged in advance.

## Delivery fee

The delivery fee depends on the weight and size of the package and on the
destination. We show the exact fee at checkout, before you finalise the order.
For larger or oversized items we provide an individual quote.

## Delivery time

- For in-stock products, typically **2–5 working days**.
- For custom-made or to-be-sourced items, we indicate the expected deadline in
  the confirmation.

## Pickup and payment

We send a confirmation e-mail about the order. In the case of **bank transfer**
we start shipping once the amount has arrived; with **card payment** after a
successful transaction. On receipt, please check that the package is intact.

## Have a question?

For questions about shipping, please reach our staff using the contact details
on the [contact page](/#kapcsolat).
